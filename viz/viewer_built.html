Paperdolls viewer

People and vehicles are decoration: they and their motion are not model output. A region sets the fleet, trees, building styles and drive side, and a look sets the colours within it. Regional tables and colours are proposals for local people to check.
Library: vehicles (assetlib.js)
19 classes, each pre-built for the four isometric headings with only the faces turned toward the camera. Regions choose the fleet (tuk-tuks, cargo bikes, double-deckers, minibuses, trams…) and the drive side. The body or livery is the data surface. See library_catalogue.html.
Library: people (assetlib.js)
Paper dolls: flat cut-outs, one neutral paper tone, no skin tones, no walking animation. Man, woman, boy, girl, couple, parent and child, family, wheelchair user, rider. Outfits follow the region's climate (coats, sun hats, umbrellas). The garment is the data surface.
Library: buildings (buildinglib.js)
Six archetypes pre-built for four headings, sized in whole bays, with a rule-based facade grammar and regional variants. The walls are the data surface; scaffold, solar, band and marker are overlays. Footprints that are not near-rectangular or are rotated more than 12 degrees off the axes fall back to a generic extrusion and are counted above.
Library: trees (treelib.js)
Nine kinds (broadleaf, oak, plane, columnar, conifer, umbrella, palm, blossom, shrub) as low-poly foliage that takes the register's flat tones. Each region has a species mix; foliage colours and bloom come from the look, so the same tree reads differently under each look and register. Flowering trees only appear when the look has a bloom colour.
Library: roads and ground
local, avenue (cycle lanes), boulevard (tram and median), cycle track. The look sets the surface: asphalt, concrete joints, setts or earth tracks, plus pavements, kerbs, gardens, parks, plazas and water. The formal register adds a faint ground grid.
Scene schema (input)
{ area, roads:[{id, class, tram?, width?, cycle?, pts:[[x,y],…]}], buildings:[{id, footprint:[[x,y],…], levels, archetype, state, investor}], landuse:[{kind:'park'|'garden'|'square'|'water', footprint}], trees:[[x,y] or [x,y,kind,scale],…] }
Coordinates are metres in a local frame. paperdolls ingest converts an OpenStreetMap extract into this shape (and writes a report of every assumption); load the resulting scene.json with the Scene control above.
