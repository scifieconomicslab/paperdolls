Asset library: catalogue (v0.4: people, vehicles, buildings, trees, themes)

Every asset is defined once, parametrically, and pre-built for the four isometric headings. Each view carries up to three faces: left, right and top (data-face in the SVG). Paper dolls, vehicles and buildings carry a data surface (data-surface): the garment on people, the body or livery on vehicles, the walls on buildings. A simulation recolours it to show an output.
Everything regional here (fleets, climate, outfits, drive side) is a proposal to be checked with local authors. People are paper dolls with one neutral paper tone for head and limbs and no skin tones. Regional variation follows climate and the local fleet, never identity.
