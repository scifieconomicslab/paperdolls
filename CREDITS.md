## Ported deterministic tooling

The OSM ingest pipeline, the seeded construct generator, the NetLogo parity
harness and the world/snapshot checkers are ported from
`github.com/scifieconomicslab/paperdolls` (CC BY 4.0). Their behaviour is
unchanged; the skills around them are new.

## Colour and design lineage — the `colorfigures` look

The visual idea (flat light/mid/dark tones by face orientation, heavy ink
outline, warm hand-inked colour) derives from **Colorfigures, by April Zhu
(MIT)**, which builds on ISO-GLOW and Tolga Cohce's iso-figure. Keep the MIT
notice when redistributing the look.

## IBM Carbon — the `formal` look

Some neutral and data-state values follow IBM's Carbon Design System
(Apache-2.0). Values recalled from memory are marked in
`reference/render/formal.md` and must be verified against
https://carbondesignsystem.com before policy use.
